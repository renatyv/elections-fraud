Election Forensics
An independent check of official election results

Do election numbers add up?

I look for unusual patterns in official election results, one local voting area at a time. Each report explains what I found, how I checked it and what the numbers cannot tell us.

Analyses

Bulgaria — presidential runoff, 1 November 2026

Yotova 57.566%, Gyurov 39.483%. All 12,717 protocol totals reconcile. Screening of 10,656 domestic sections does not establish ballot stuffing; one digit flag and 14 protocol discrepancies remain open.

Serbia — parliamentary election, 25 October 2026

I saved 14 updates from the unfinished count, covering 8,124 polling stations, and compared them with 2023. Some totals and unusual patterns still need checking. Keeping the votes fixed, a seat calculation gives 140 seats to Students, 104 to the Vučić list and six to minorities; this is not a prediction.

Brazil — presidential runoff, 25 October 2026

Eleven groups of checks on 499,248 polling sections found no clear evidence of manipulation. I compared the results with the 2022 runoff and saved election-night updates. Estimates of where votes went between rounds have limits: area totals cannot reveal individual choices.

Slovakia — municipal & regional elections, 24 October 2026

Ten groups of checks on 6,029 regional and 6,024 local polling stations found no clear signs of manipulation, using 2022 for comparison. Local races had 79,290 more participants than regional races. That gap alone does not tell us why.

Slovenia — four referendums, 11 October 2026

Eight groups of checks on 2,984 polling stations, each with four ballots, found no clear signs of manipulation. The totals match those published on Wikipedia exactly. The report also shows how counting areas instead of votes could change the result.

Portugal — legislative elections, 18 May 2025

Ten groups of checks on 3,092 parishes found no clear signs of manipulation. Polling-station totals in six municipalities match the official figures exactly. The voting rules matter too: PS won more votes than CH but fewer seats.

United States — Senate and House, 5 November 2024

284 checks of vote-count digits, county turnout comparisons and ten races where other candidates’ votes outnumbered the winner’s lead. Ohio’s unusual digit pattern needs a closer look. The data cover only part of the country and cannot settle whether fraud occurred.

India — Lok Sabha, April–June 2024

The checks found no clear signs of manipulation in the available data: 542 constituencies, 646 million ballots and 109,952 Uttar Pradesh polling stations recovered from scanned Form 20 tally sheets. I used 2014 for comparison. Missing local data limit what I can conclude.
